## 1. Introduction

Welcome to AppLock, a security utility developed by SK Software.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information AppLock accesses, how that information is used, where it is stored, and how third-party advertising services may process certain device-related information.

AppLock is designed so that its core security functionality operates locally on your device.

## 2. What AppLock Does

AppLock allows you to protect selected applications on your Android device using a PIN or your device's supported biometric authentication, such as fingerprint or face authentication.

When a protected application is opened, AppLock displays its security screen and requires successful authentication before access is allowed.

## 3. Information We Collect

AppLock does not collect or transmit your security information to our servers.

The following information is handled locally on your device:

- Your AppLock PIN
- Your list of protected applications
- Local unlock/activity records maintained by the application

Your PIN is stored in a cryptographically protected form using a salted PBKDF2 hash. AppLock does not store your PIN as plain text.

AppLock does not access, store, or transmit your actual fingerprint or facial biometric data.

## 4. Android Permissions and Access

AppLock requires certain Android permissions and system capabilities to provide its security features.

### Accessibility Service

AppLock uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect when applications are opened so that protected applications can be secured.

The service is configured only for the functionality required by AppLock. AppLock does not intentionally read or collect the contents of your screen, passwords, messages, keyboard input, or other private content.

The Accessibility Service is configured with `canRetrieveWindowContent` disabled, meaning AppLock does not request access to window content.

### Usage Statistics

AppLock may use Android Usage Stats as a secondary method for determining application foreground status and supporting its app-locking functionality.

### Installed Applications

AppLock requires access to the installed application list so that it can display applications available for protection and allow you to select which applications should be locked.

### Biometric Authentication

AppLock can use Android's system biometric authentication framework to allow you to unlock protected applications using supported fingerprint or face authentication.

AppLock does not receive or store your actual biometric information. Biometric verification is handled by the Android operating system and compatible device hardware.

### Internet and Network Access

Internet access is required for displaying advertisements provided through Google AdMob.

AppLock's core security data is not transmitted to SK Software servers.

## 5. Accessibility Service Privacy

AppLock uses the Android Accessibility Service only to identify when a protected application is brought to the foreground and to provide the app-locking experience.

AppLock does not intentionally collect:

- Screen contents
- Private messages
- Passwords
- Keyboard input
- Personal conversations
- Photos or videos from other applications

The Accessibility Service is not used to collect or transmit personal content to SK Software.

## 6. Local Data Storage and Security

Information created by AppLock is stored locally on your Android device.

This may include:

- Your protected application settings
- Your cryptographically protected PIN information
- Local unlock/activity records
- Application preferences and settings

Your security information is not uploaded to SK Software servers.

If you uninstall AppLock, Android removes the application's locally stored data. You may also use the **Reset All Data** option available in AppLock Settings to remove the application's locally stored data.

## 7. Data Transmission

AppLock does not transmit your PIN, protected application list, biometric information, or other core security data to SK Software servers.

However, AppLock displays advertisements using Google AdMob. As part of providing advertising services, Google and its advertising partners may process certain device-related and advertising-related information.

## 8. Third-Party Advertising — Google AdMob

AppLock uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

Google may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, and other information permitted for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and related purposes.

Depending on applicable laws, device settings, consent status, and Google's advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google's processing of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google handles information for advertising, please refer to Google's Privacy & Terms and advertising privacy information.

## 9. Children's Privacy

AppLock is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our own servers.

Advertising services provided by Google may apply additional protections and restrictions where required by applicable law and Google's policies.

## 10. Data Deletion

You can delete AppLock's locally stored application data by using the **Reset All Data** option in the application Settings.

Uninstalling AppLock also removes its locally stored application data from your device, subject to normal Android system behavior.

Advertising-related information processed by Google is controlled according to Google's policies and applicable device and account settings. You may manage relevant advertising settings through your Android/Google settings.

## 11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures within the application to protect the security information stored locally by AppLock.

Your PIN is not stored as plain text. Instead, AppLock uses a cryptographically salted PBKDF2 hash for PIN verification.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also protect their device with a secure screen lock and keep Android and the AppLock application updated.
